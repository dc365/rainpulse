# X polar morphology: complete original parent history

The angular width limit is a qualification limit, not a measurement filter.
Every measurable original envelope must enter its complete radial history,
including an envelope wider than the configured qualification limit. A later
narrow tail must not restart because its wide predecessor was omitted.

Qualification retains the existing width, physical span, original support,
bilateral measured shoulder, unknown-gap and boundary-history requirements.
Oversize predecessors cannot confer eligibility or source identity on children.
Unrelated measured objects remain independently eligible; there is no global
sweep veto. North-seam adjacency, native angular footprints and declared gaps
retain their existing semantics. Work/object/gate budgets remain unchanged and
resource exhaustion remains atomic. No RAW mutation or weather-truth claim.

This applies to every station, scan time, elevation and original sampling,
without station/azimuth/time-specific conditions. It does not by itself resolve
an interference band mixed into a broad weather object; independent original
subband boundaries and shoulder/source evidence are still required.
