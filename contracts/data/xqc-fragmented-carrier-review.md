# X fragmented carrier review v3

This is a default-off, offline candidate increment. It is not a weather truth
classifier, confirmed RF interference, or an operational product promotion.

The morphology detector may export exact observed members of each **complete
qualified original track**, including members withheld by its compact-contour
proxy. Export does not change the original masks, object IDs or dispositions.
Every track keeps its original adverse, missing and branching history; export
must not reconstruct membership from a rectangular bounding box.

Only compact members of an exported qualified carrier may be reconsidered.
For each native ray and physical target range block, exclude the entire target
block and one adjacent block on either side from reference measurements. At
least three other blocks spanning 20 km must have measured SNR on that same
original carrier. Reference and target availability must be at least 80%.
Use the nearest held-out blocks until both support conditions are met, including
all equal-distance ties. Full original geometry remains the nomination basis;
the field stencil does not restart the original track. The target's measured
90th percentile must be within 1 dB of the median of the reference blocks'
**90th percentiles**. Comparing a target upper quantile with reference medians
would treat ordinary stationary native variability as a local enhancement.
These are the existing 5 km / three block / 20 km
receiver distances and 1 dB source-fan membership margin; they do not assert a
universal range response or estimate missing values.

Local signal excess and insufficient evidence retain their separate review
masks. Hard, local and external weather protections remain authoritative.
No returned member may be missing, protected or outside its original carrier.
RAW arrays and compact diagnostics are immutable. Review does not modify a
completed parent action; integration through the sole writer is a separate gate.

The original morphology work plus exact membership and held-out work share the
unchanged 50 million work cap and two million gate cap. Exhaustion returns no
partial increment. Exported memberships are immutable and charged to that cap.
The detector is not called by the production pipeline until a versioned policy,
native product proof and representative weather controls are accepted.
