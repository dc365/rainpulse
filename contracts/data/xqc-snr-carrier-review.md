# Original SNR carrier review (candidate only)

This review nominates original polar members independently of DBZH threshold
contours. DBZH can merge neighboring source lanes into an unstable envelope;
that does not erase a measured SNR lane's complete geometry. Geometry is a
nomination, not verified RF interference or independent rainfall truth.

The policy explicitly declares SNR levels in dB. They are not reflectivity
thresholds or implicit copies of a station's DBZH levels. The existing complete
history engine may receive an internal geometric signal view, but RAW moments,
coordinates, availability, no-echo state and acquisition row identity are never
changed. Neither velocity nor phase extension codes are needed for this review.

Targets require original legal DBZH availability, an observed echo at or above
5 dBZ and available SNR. Only exact members of a qualified complete original
SNR history are nominated. Missing SNR never supplies a quiet receiver vote.
Native time/elevation/azimuth gaps, missing shoulders, curved and narrowing
weather histories retain their existing geometry refusals. Explicit protection
always excludes targets. Local proxy policy cannot be relaxed by this review.

The initial held-out support uses original 5 km range blocks, three or more
nonadjacent reference blocks spanning at least 20 km, target/neighbor guards,
and every tie at the stopping distance. Target and reference use the same SNR
p90 statistic. Power above the reference median by more than 1 dB remains local
excess; below by more than 1 dB or unavailable support remains unknown. These
are independently declared, frozen candidate parameters, not a fitted weather
model. An overlapping narrower identity cannot override excess or unknown from
another original history.

All geometry and profile work share one unchanged bounded review allowance.
Native gates, object count and summary bytes are bounded before allocation;
row scans, partitions, percentiles, sorting and comparisons are charged. A
resource refusal returns no partial review. Results retain candidate, excess,
unknown, exact original carrier count and work, with RAW digest unchanged.

This primitive has no product writer, automatic activation, release or QPE
authority. An optional `snr_carrier_policy` (default null) selects the prepared
normal integration. Its geometry must equal the existing morphology contract;
explicit SNR levels remain separate. Full receiver-family support and complete
morphology protection are prerequisites. The integration uses the existing sole X writer,
candidate action 3, parent proposals and budget reviews, native evidence export,
and explicit protection/action/evidence refusal states. SNR has its own candidate,
excess, unknown masks and state (1 evaluated, 2 protection/source unavailable,
3 resource refusal, 4 action allowance refusal, 5 evidence refusal). Disabled
calls retain legacy parameter digest, implementation identity and arrays.
Enabling it requires
source-bound actual pipeline and map verification. A successful synthetic or
source-membership check is not weather-loss acceptance or complete radial/fan
quality acceptance. Finite transient strong residuals remain a separate open
acceptance item; they must not be hidden by widening tolerances or deleting all
local excess.
